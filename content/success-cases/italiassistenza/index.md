## Human AssistCare

<div class="case-division-stat">Since 2006</div>

Since 2006, it has been the Italian leader in designing and delivering patient support services — **Patient Support Programs (PSPs)** and **home clinical trials** — activated **in partnership with pharmaceutical and biotech companies, medical device manufacturers, and CROs**. PSPs are integrated physical and digital services designed to assist patients in therapy and their caregivers, aimed at improving therapeutic adherence and quality of life. They support the care pathway without replacing the National Health Service (SSN) and can include:

* Nursing and psychological support

* Technical assistance

* Educational pathways

* Home telediagnostics services

* Drug delivery

* Collection and disposal of medical waste

* Monitoring apps for the patient and their physician

Through two operational centers (one operating 24/7), a nationwide capillary network of over 100 healthcare professionals, and a technology platform, more than 20,000 home visits and over 100,000 phone contacts are organized per year.

## The Collaboration in Brief

**Mia-Care** collaborated with ItaliAssistenza to develop a new white-label, cloud-native, modular microservices portal for managing services across the Human Assistcare and Careapt divisions. The portal ensures compliance with General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), HIPAA, and international cybersecurity policies, enabling the secure and flexible configuration of data and operational flows.

This infrastructure guarantees **seamless interoperability with existing internal and third-party systems** through a documented API portal. The orchestration of multiple dedicated digital touchpoints synergistically involves all stakeholders: Operational Center operators, field operators, logistics providers, as well as patients, physicians, and partner companies. Furthermore, the solution is designed to support the development of software equivalent to and compliant with medical device standards.

## ItaliAssistenza's Business Model: Enhancing Integrated Care with Technology

To deliver informational, organizational, and technical support services to patients, caregivers, and hospital physicians, ItaliAssistenza provides a structured system of home, telephone, and digital services. It adopts an operational model based on an **integrated, multidisciplinary approach** leveraging the following **strategic assets**:

* A healthcare operational center supported by a multidisciplinary team

* Two non-clinical operational centers, one of which operates 24/7

* A field network of healthcare professionals

* The strategic and constant use of technology

ItaliAssistenza continuously seeks **initiatives to improve and innovate its services** to maximize end-user satisfaction and streamline its operational model by increasing productivity and optimizing costs, thereby maintaining a competitive market advantage.

In this context, the need emerged to develop a **new state-of-the-art technological and digital platform** conceived to ensure synergistic, highly capillary management of all involved stakeholders: care recipients, caregivers, physicians, and operators.

The platform integrates various functionalities designed for continuous care, such as remote diagnosis and telemonitoring, leveraging digital channels (web portals and apps) to further increase the overall effectiveness and quality of offered services.

## The Challenge: ItaliAssistenza’s Roadmap Towards a Digital Ecosystem

Industry demands, the pressing need to ensure data processing security and continuity, and the broad range of offered services led ItaliAssistenza to establish fundamental milestones for digitalizing its systems.

The main problem lay in managing **dozens of different Patient Support Programs (PSPs)**, each with its **dedicated CRM**. Every instance entailed different configurations, different workflows, and separate databases, negatively impacting both operations and business:

* **Operations:** No scalability or component reuse, high setup costs, extended onboarding times, lack of resource governance, vendor lock-in for reporting and changes, and complex integration with third-party systems.

* **Business:** Slow setup, high recurring costs, and low competitiveness.

## ItaliAssistenza’s Digitalization Objectives

To overcome this fragmentation, ItaliAssistenza set key objectives towards digitalizing and centralizing its systems:

1. **IT Infrastructure Enhancement:** Develop a cloud-based platform with a robust, secure, and scalable IT architecture capable of evolving over time.

2. **Holistic Stakeholder Management:** Ensure integrated management of all actors involved in service delivery (care recipients, caregivers, physicians, and operators) by providing cutting-edge features like remote diagnosis and telemonitoring.

3. **Patient Journey Personalization:** Create integrated, easily adaptable Patient Journeys with a meticulously designed user experience for end users.

4. **Operational Process Automation and Efficiency:** Digitalize key processes such as assignment allocation to the healthcare network, traceability of rendered services, and archiving of care pathway data and documentation.

* An application module dedicated to services delivered within PSPs.

## Impact of the New Platform

ItaliAssistenza identified the implementation of the new platform as a key strategic initiative within its business transformation, requiring a robust change management system.

The PSP platform digital transformation project generated the following outcomes and benefits:

* **Privacy and Security by Design:** Security measures are built directly into the software's foundation rather than added later. Adopting a security-by-design approach, information protection and compliance with major international standards (such as GDPR and HIPAA) are integral to the software architecture from inception, ensuring native data partitioning and preventing retrospective fixes.

* **Compliance and Quality Assurance:** The solution actively supports ItaliAssistenza in meeting strict industry quality standards, such as ISO 9001 and ISO 27001. Additionally, the platform architecture is compliance-ready, embedding technical requirements for software development compliant with IEC 62304 and ISO 13485 standards for medical devices.

* **Operational Efficiency:** Streamlined, integrated, and significantly simplified operational workflows.

* **Platform Optimization and Scalability:** The platform is optimized to guarantee scalability and support future evolutions.

* **Centralization and Personalization:** Enhanced capacity to customize and centralize performance visualization for Patient Support Programs.

* **Improved Experience:** Enhanced user experience for both end users and field operators, contributing to higher care quality.

* **Business Acceleration:** The platform enables rapid activation of new partnerships and projects, facilitating customization based on specific pharma client requirements.

* **Expanded Offering Portfolio:** The solution is pre-configured to ingest data from third-party services, easing integration and transmission between ItaliAssistenza and field operators.

* **Time to Market:** The modular, white-label solution ensures low activation times for new services or contracts, delivering a real competitive market advantage.
