We are looking for a Software Engineer with a strong engineering mindset, passionate about building scalable, high-quality cloud-native products in the healthcare domain, with a focus on Software as a Medical Device (SaMD). The passion for Artificial Intelligence and a keen interest in integrating AI into the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) are highly valued.

You will work in a cross-functional team (PM, Designers, Developers), collaborating end-to-end on the design, implementation, and deployment of features used by thousands of developers every day, contributing to products that support clinical workflows and patient-centric solutions in a regulated environment.

## Activities
- Design, develop, and maintain software with a focus on scalability, reliability, and performance, ensuring healthcare-grade quality and safety standards
- Contribute to frontend development using React
- Participate in architectural decisions and the design of new components, APIs, and integrations, considering data privacy, security, and regulatory constraints (e.g., GDPR)
- Collaborate with multiple teams to deliver product features end-to-end
- Write and maintain tests, monitor services in production, and help improve product quality
- Take part in code reviews and promote engineering best practices
- Contribute to the evolution of our Platform for Software as a Medical Device development (P4SaMD)
- Explore and implement opportunities to integrate AI tools and practices into the team's SDLC

## Required Skills
- 3+ years of experience in software development
- Solid knowledge of at least one backend technology: Node.js (JavaScript/TypeScript), Go, or Rust
- Experience with React on the frontend
- Ability to identify and integrate relevant AI technologies to improve software development processes
- Good understanding of microservices architectures, REST APIs, and event-driven systems
- Experience with Docker and CI/CD pipelines
- Comfortable using Git and collaborating within a development team
- Interest or experience in healthcare, medical software, or regulated environments (e.g., SaMD, ISO 13485, IEC 62304)

## Nice-to-Have
- Experience with Kubernetes
- Experience with Kafka and event-driven architecture
- Familiarity with observability practices: logging, metrics, monitoring
- Familiarity with healthcare interoperability standards (e.g., HL7, FHIR)
- Experience working on regulated software (medical devices, fintech, or similar domains)
- Knowledge of secure handling of sensitive data and privacy-by-design principles
- Interest in Developer Experience, Platform Engineering, or automation
- Product-oriented mindset and focus on delivering value
