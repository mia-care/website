Mia-Care is building the AI-native software platform for medical device software lifecycle management, right where AI meets medical device regulation. We're a startup, so there's no playbook: we're writing it. If blank-slate problems energize you and "we've always done it this way" feels unsettling, you'll fit right in. This open application goes straight to our People and Culture team, who will get in touch as soon as an opportunity matching your background and aspirations opens up. Missed us at the Career Day or found us somewhere else? You're welcome to apply here too.

## Potential Roles & Areas of Interest
- Engineering & Tech: Fullstack and Product Engineering (Node, TypeScript, Java, Go, React/Angular), Cloud Architecture (Kubernetes, Kafka, CI/CD), Data & AI (Python, Rust, RAG)
- Product, Design & Content: Product Management, UX/UI Design, Product Marketing
- Business, Delivery & Operations: Technical Pre-Sales, Product Owner, Customer Success, Sales

## What We Look For
- Academic background: enrolled student or recent graduate in Computer Science, Management Engineering, Mathematical Engineering, Design, or related fields
- Tech passion: strong interest in Cloud Native technologies, modern software architectures, and digital and AI innovation
- Mindset: a proactive approach, problem-solving skills, and a strong desire to learn and adapt quickly
- Soft skills: excellent relational skills, a teamwork mentality, and precision
- Languages: proficient English. Italian is optional for most technical and product roles, but welcome

## How You Can Join Us
- Flexible collaboration tailored to your studies and career stage: curricular and extracurricular internships, or full-time entry-level contracts with competitive packages and comprehensive benefits

## Smart Hybrid Work (50/50)
- A standard baseline of 50% in the office and 50% remote, combining in-person collaboration with remote autonomy

- Our Milan HQ is built for focus, collaboration and fun: great workstations, ping-pong, games and lounge areas, two fully stocked kitchens with free snacks and drinks, a rooftop terrace and beer on tap after 6:30 PM
