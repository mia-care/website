As an AI Engineer, you'll be at the core of what makes Mia-Care tick. Our platform uses LLMs to analyze medical device software, identify compliance gaps, and suggest actionable remediations. You'll own the intelligence layer that makes this possible — from model selection and fine-tuning to production deployment and cost optimization.

This isn't a role where you plug in an API and call it a day. You'll be shaping how AI works inside a highly specialized, high-stakes domain, where quality and reliability are non-negotiable.

## Activities
- Design, fine-tune, and evaluate LLMs for domain-specific tasks in medical device software analysis
- Craft and validate prompts that drive gap analysis and remediation workflows — iterating rigorously until the output is production-grade
- Own the full MLOps lifecycle: model versioning, monitoring, evaluation pipelines, and continuous improvement
- Optimize for cost and scalability — obsessing over inference efficiency, latency, and growth without burning the budget
- Build AI pipelines that are reliable, observable, and production-grade from day one
- Collaborate directly with product and domain experts to translate regulatory complexity into AI-solvable problems

## How We Work
- Full-stack AI mindset — you own your work end-to-end, from research and prototyping to deployment and monitoring
- Practical engineering — we prioritize clean, maintainable code and automation to keep infrastructure robust and scalable
- High ownership, low bureaucracy — we give you the autonomy to make critical technical decisions and expect a strong commitment to excellence

## Required Skills
- Master's Degree in Computer Science or Computer Engineering
- 0–2+ years of relevant experience
- Knowledge of Python or Rust
- Knowledge of LLM tools (LangChain, OpenAI / Anthropic / open-source model ecosystem)
- Familiarity with MLOps tools and practices: Docker, GCP (Vertex AI, Cloud Run, GCS)
- Experience with LLM evaluation frameworks, prompt versioning, and model monitoring
- Experience with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines
