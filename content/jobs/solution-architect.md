We are looking for a Solution Architect who can create the overall vision and strategy for a specific solution to a business problem or need. You will design, describe, and manage the solution, acting as a bridge between a business problem and the technology solution. You will help both potential and existing customers understand and implement their internal solutions using Mia-Platform's and Mia-Care's products.

## Activities
- Work with potential and existing customers to understand their needs and outline how Mia-Platform solutions can drive their digital transformation
- Guide customers in reducing cloud-native complexity, establishing architecture guidelines and simplifying infrastructure management through our Internal Developer Portal (IDP)
- Design modern software architectures for customer Digital Platforms using core runtime components (e.g., Fast Data, API Gateway, Backoffice, Flow Manager)
- Assist customer engineering teams in designing Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) solutions, leveraging Mia-Care's dedicated frameworks and Mia-Platform's developer tools
- Define and implement hands-on POCs on our platform to solve real customer challenges
- Collaborate cross-functionally with R&D, Delivery and Sales teams to bring market feedback into the continuous evolution of our product suite
- Work closely with Mia-Care partners to help them define scalable, high-quality architectures for their clients

## Required Skills
- Master's Degree in Computer Science or Computer Engineering
- Strong hands-on experience with Cloud-Native technologies (Kubernetes, Microservices, API Management) and modern software lifecycle practices
- Understanding of DevOps methods, CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure automation tools
- Proven track record in designing and delivering custom enterprise software solutions
- Ability to bridge business needs and complex technology: running hands-on POCs, untangling client constraints and winning over technical stakeholders
- Excellent relationship-building skills with both business and technical peers
- Fluent English, written and spoken
- A curious, passionate learner who enjoys reverse engineering legacy setups and staying ahead of technology trends (AI, Data Fabric, etc.)
- Knowledge of or previous exposure to the medical device and health-tech regulatory framework (e.g., ISO 13485, IEC 62304) is a plus

## Why Mia-Care
- Mia-Care is building the AI-native software platform for medical device software lifecycle management. We sit at the intersection of two of the most demanding fields in tech, AI and medical device regulation, and we're building the infrastructure that will shape how this industry works for years to come
- We're a startup, so there's no playbook: we're writing it. If blank-slate problems energize you, if you'd rather architect a solution than maintain one, and if "we've always done it this way" genuinely bothers you, you'll fit right in
- Our culture is built on Better Development, and that applies to our people as much as to our software
