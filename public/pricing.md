# Pricing — P4SaMD by Mia-Care

> P4SaMD does not publish numeric list prices. All tiers are quoted per engagement — contact sales@mia-care.io or request a demo at https://mia-care.io/request-demo. This file exists so AI agents and automated tools can read tier structure and features without rendering the page.

## Standard
- Price: Custom — contact sales
- Target: Small teams getting started
- Limits: 1 company · 3 products · 5 users
- Features: SDLC Workflow Orchestrator, Requirements & Risk Management, Basic Verification & Validation framework, Documentation Engine (standard templates), BOM & Change Management, Limited AI/ML Compliance Pack, on-demand support

## Professional
- Price: Custom — contact sales
- Target: Growing teams scaling compliance
- Limits: 1 company · 5 products · 10 users
- Features: Everything in Standard, plus tailored Smart Insight & AI Assistant, document templating system, full AI/ML Compliance Pack, legacy systems assessment, pre-validation package, integrations & MCP Server, ticketing support

## Unlimited
- Price: Custom — contact sales
- Target: Enterprises & multi-product organizations
- Limits: Unlimited companies, products & users
- Features: Everything in Professional, plus AI-assisted legacy remediation, eQMS integration, support for audit readiness, 24/7 ticketing with dedicated lead & AI assistant

## Deployment models (available on all tiers)
- SaaS: fully managed cloud deployment, instant provisioning, automatic updates
- Self-Hosted: private cloud or on-premise, for data residency, sovereign cloud, or network isolation requirements

Full tier comparison: https://mia-care.io/plans
